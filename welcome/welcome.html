3. What your buddy does

  • Sip: it says "Cheers!", raises its bottle to you, hops and walks on. Your sip is logged and the next reminder is a full interval away.
  • Snooze: it gets angry, with a big anger mark, and stomps back the way it came. It's back after your snooze time (3 minutes unless you change it).
  • Ignore it: after 60 seconds it snoozes by itself, just as angry.
